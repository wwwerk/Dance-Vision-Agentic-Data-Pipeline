# Gold-set plan

## Initial target

Build a carefully reviewed set of approximately 30–90 minutes of selected video, with 30–60 especially valuable clips, rather than attempting exhaustive annotation of many hours.

## Coverage goals

- Solo, duet, small group, and dense group scenes
- Easy, moderate, and high occlusion
- Upright work, floor work, inversions, lifts, and contact
- Different lighting/camera conditions
- Multiple sessions and performer groups where rights permit

## Quality requirements

- Session-aware split assignment
- Reviewer status recorded
- Difficult intervals adjudicated or flagged
- Export validation passed
- Source, run, and annotation lineage complete

## Exclusion policy

Exclude or separately classify material with unresolved rights, extreme visual ambiguity, unmanageable privacy risk, or annotations too uncertain for the intended benchmark.
