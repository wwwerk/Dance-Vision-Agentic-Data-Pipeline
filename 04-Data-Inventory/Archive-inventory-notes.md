# Archive inventory notes

## Initial archive profile

The broader archive contains performing arts, dance, contact improvisation, yoga instruction, single-camera recordings, and professional multi-camera captures. The first pipeline should use a small selected subset rather than attempting broad processing.

## Sampling dimensions

- Solo / duet / small group / dense group
- Number of visible bodies
- Inter-person physical contact
- Self-occlusion and inter-person occlusion
- Floor work / upright movement / inversion / lifts
- Camera angle, distance, and stability
- Lighting quality and motion blur
- Costume/background complexity
- Movement style and setting
- Rights/consent status

## Initial target sample

Select at least 20 candidates, then create short representative clips from 5–10 of them for the first vertical slice.
