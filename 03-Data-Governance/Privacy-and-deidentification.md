# Privacy and de-identification

## Risks

Dance, yoga, workshop, and contact-improvisation material can reveal identity, health/mobility information, intimate interpersonal interaction, location, or sensitive community context.

## Mitigations

- Use only rights-cleared material for the intended purpose.
- Prefer skeleton renders, abstract motion plots, blurred faces, cropped regions, or synthetic examples for public demonstration.
- Keep personal names and direct identifiers out of public manifests.
- Restrict raw video access and avoid broad cloud sharing links.
- Provide a traceable removal/withdrawal path for material that is eligible for removal.
- Distinguish public metadata from restricted internal metadata.

## Public-release review

Before release, verify:

- [ ] Rights and consent status
- [ ] Performer/bystander privacy
- [ ] Location and contextual metadata
- [ ] License compatibility
- [ ] Whether derivatives remain identifiable
- [ ] Whether a demonstration can use skeleton output instead
