# Dataset card draft

## Dataset name

Dance Occlusion and Interaction Dataset — working title

## Summary

A planned dataset of selected human-movement video clips with multi-person 2D pose, persistent identity, visibility/occlusion, and temporal tracking annotations. The dataset focuses on difficult movement conditions including physical contact, overlap, floor work, unusual articulation, and extended temporal continuity.

## Intended use

- Research on occlusion-aware 2D pose estimation
- Multi-person pose tracking and identity continuity
- Human-motion analysis and video-indexing prototypes
- Annotation and active-learning workflow evaluation

## Out-of-scope use

- Identification, surveillance, or biometric profiling
- Health, diagnostic, or clinical inference
- Unreviewed commercial use of restricted source content
- Any use conflicting with source-specific performer restrictions

## Known limitations

- Dataset may not represent all bodies, movement traditions, camera setups, or lighting conditions
- Source and release rights may limit public availability
